Gift trial assets — Jopi

Extract the gift-trial folder into your project public folder:
G:/jopi/jopi/public/gift-trial/

Contains seven distinct gifts: grand banquet, rocket, Christmas gift bag, fireworks, cherry blossom ice cream, love rocket, music box.
Alternate formats and sizes of the same gift are not duplicated. Avatar frames, profile wings, badges and room entrance banners are excluded.

Video conversion: the RGB and alpha rectangles from each original file/configuration were combined into WebM alpha. The MOV animation was converted with its existing alpha. Original artwork and watermarks were retained. Native WebM, SVGA and PAG are also included.

Playback libraries (loaded locally only on demand): SVGAPlayer-Web 2.3.2 and libpag 4.3.51. Library licenses are included. These software licenses do not establish commercial rights to the gift artwork. User supplied demonstration assets remain subject to their original terms.

The Trial tab is a free local preview. No sending, wallet deduction or room broadcast is performed. Existing gift sending is unchanged.
